The Little Garden

Maya loved to visit her grandmother's garden every summer. Tall sunflowers
stood along the fence, turning their bright yellow faces toward the sun.
Bees moved quietly from flower to flower, and a small stream ran along the
edge of the yard. Every morning, Maya would water the plants and pull out
any weeds she found. Her grandmother said that a garden, like a good
reader, grows a little more every single day.
